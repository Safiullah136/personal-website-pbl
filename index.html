About me

I am an undergraduate student in computer science interested in building efficient, reliable, and verifiable computer systems. My interests lie at the intersection of systems, programming languages, and formal methods.

I like to study system abstractions, runtime mechanisms, and hardware-software interactions, and how they affect performance. Right now I'm digging into how to analyze the performance of large-scale, complex systems, and how formal methods can help with that analysis.

Outside of computing, I enjoy cooking. I speak Sindhi and English.

MapReduce

Implemented MapReduce from the original paper. Workers request map and reduce tasks from a master, and map output is partitioned by key hash into reduce buckets. The master reassigns any task that runs past 10 seconds, and workers exit when the master is gone.
